What if I don’t prequalify?
If you don’t prequalify for a grooming appointment, don’t worry! This could be due to factors like your dog’s health, vaccination status, or specific grooming requirements. Contact us, and we’ll work with you to find a solution, recommend alternative services, or help you meet the necessary requirements for a future appointment. Your pup’s well-being is our priority!
Can I prequalify over the phone?

A: Yes, you can! Feel free to call us and discuss your dog’s grooming needs. We’ll be happy to guide you through our services, answer any questions, and help you choose the right grooming package for your pup.

Can I send a money order or check?

We currently accept payments via credit/debit cards, cash, and online payment methods. Unfortunately, we do not accept money orders or checks. If you have any questions or need assistance with payment options, feel free to contact us!
