# Hybrid V2: decisions and content model

Isolated concept prototype. Nothing here changes grooming-dogs.com.

## 1. What V1 got right, what it got wrong

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep** | Answer before article. V1 puts a usable answer on the first screen and ends every guide with a next step. |
| **Most consequential weakness** | V1 runs on a parallel content model the real site does not have: 30 coat-by-task answers and 12 invented guides at `#g-…` addresses. It could not ship without inventing pages. It also makes coat the gate for everything, while most real questions (teeth, ears, cost, nerves, problems after grooming) do not depend on coat, and only 5 of the 17 real guides (bathing, brushing, nails, ears, teeth) had an equivalent in its finder. |
| **The trade-off that matters** | Instant, specific answers versus truth to the real pages. V2 takes answers only from what is published: each page's short answer, its real comparison tables, its published questions and its real section anchors. Coat becomes one lens, used only where the real content changes by coat (identification, schedule, starter kit). |

## 2. Structure V2 uses

**Split stage.** The page is a publication on the left and a desk on the right.

- The **publication** (scrolling) leads: the real home headline, then the five chapters as a contents spread, then
  the real comparisons, the symptom finder, the coat lens, and how the guide works.
- The **desk** (sticky on desktop, a sheet on phones) supports: an intake card with search, and a live preview of
  whatever guide you point at (its published short answer, its sections as deep links, the guides it links to).

Search sits on the desk because the desk is always in reach; it is the first control on the first screen.

## 3. Five clusters mapped to the 22 published pages

Clusters are a content model, not navigation. The live menu stays as it is (Grooming tasks · Your dog · Problems ·
Tools & groomers).

| Cluster | Pages (real URLs) |
| --- | --- |
| Understand Your Dog | `/dog-coat-types/` · `/how-often-should-you-groom-your-dog/` |
| Everyday Grooming | `/how-to-groom-your-dog-at-home/` · `/bathing-your-dog/` · `/brushing-your-dog/` · `/trimming-dog-nails/` · `/cleaning-dog-ears/` · `/brushing-dog-teeth/` |

| Outside the clusters | `/` · `/about/` · `/editorial-policy/` · `/contact/` · `/privacy-policy/` |

**How destinations were verified:** URLs, titles, section anchors, questions, tables and the internal-link graph are
generated by `tools/extract_content.py` from `BUILD/out/` (the Elementor data that was published) and its manifest;
the readiness report records all 22 pages published on 2026-10-05 with 0 post-publish link issues. The live site was
**not** fetched from this environment, so a live link check is still owed before any of this ships.

## 4. Gaps and open questions (not guessed)

- **No live search exists.** The header has no search. The prototype searches a static index built from the build
  records. Production path: generate the same index at publish time (JSON beside the theme) or use WordPress's
  native `/?s=` results page; neither is set up today.
- **Shedding and mats have no pages.** They live as sections of `/brushing-your-dog/` (`#managing-shedding`,
  `#dealing-with-mats`). V2 links to those anchors and does not invent pages.
- **Glossary placement** is a judgement call (Understand vs Tools & Technique); V2 puts it in Tools & Technique.
- **Cost page is US-only** (prices from US businesses); the prototype shows them as published.
- **Images:** the live photos are on the site and not reachable here, so V2 uses no photographs. Real photography is
  the first thing to add.
- **Reference galleries** (Minimal Gallery, Godly, Awwwards and the rest) were blocked by this environment's network
  policy, so they could not be browsed; their known patterns informed the split stage, contents spread and
  comparison tabs.
